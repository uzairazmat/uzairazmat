## About

**Uzair Azmat is an AI Product Engineer based in Lahore, Pakistan.** He builds and ships production AI products, not demos, taking ideas from POC to MVP to live systems across backend, AI, and deployment.

At **CWIT (Clear Wave Information Technology)** he designs **conversational AI systems, voice AI platforms, AI copilots, RAG assistants, and multi-agent workflows** used in real business workflows, with a focus on **low latency, reliability, cost optimization, and user experience**.

**What I do**

- 🤖 **AI copilots:** LLM assistants connected to internal knowledge bases that automate replies and **reduce support response time**

- 🧠 **Multi-agent workflows:** safety gates, specialist agents, tool use, and booking/CRM-style integrations
- 💸 **Cost and performance optimization:** the simplest model and architecture that meets the quality bar, with monitoring on latency, accuracy, and spend
- 📈 **LLMOps and MLOps:** versioning, automated evals, tracing, and experiment tracking (MLflow, DVC, DagsHub, Weights & Biases)
- ⚙️ **Async infrastructure:** multi-worker background pipelines for high-volume AI tasks
