## About

Most AI demos work. Fewer survive real callers, real documents, and real concurrency.

I'm a Backend & AI Engineer who ships **production-grade conversational AI**: voice bots that take thousands of live calls, and copilots that answer from a company's own knowledge base. I own the whole path, from architecture and retrieval strategy to evaluation, deployment, and tracing, and I know when a simpler approach beats a bigger model.

**What I actually do:**

- 🎙️ Build **voice AI platforms** (inbound and outbound) on real-time streaming pipelines with LiveKit
- 🤖 Ship **AI copilots** that plug LLMs into internal knowledge bases to cut support response time
- 📚 Design **RAG backends** with pgvector and ChromaDB that stay accurate across thousands of documents
- 🧠 Orchestrate **multi-agent workflows** with safety gates, specialist agents, and tool/booking integrations
- ⚙️ Run **async, multi-worker pipelines** for high-volume AI workloads
- 📈 Treat **evals and LLMOps** (versioning, tracing, automated evaluation) as part of the product

Currently working full-time as a Backend & AI Engineer at **CWIT** while completing my BSCS.

## Engineering Principles

- Build fewer things. Build them right.
- Measure before you ship: latency, accuracy, cost, and call quality.
- Use the simplest approach that works. Reach for agents when they earn their place.
- Ownership from requirements to production.
- Evals and tracing are part of the product, not an afterthought.
